SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Flexibility: I am flexible person in terms of studying and working.
  • Persistence: If I have goal, I am determined to achieve it despite all the difficulties.
  • Curiosity: I’m genuinely interested in this world, and I’m always craving for new knowledge.

Weaknesses

  • I tend to downplay my achievements and capabilities, which sometimes really demotivates me.
  • I’m introverted person and sometimes I might avoid too much social contact which can influence my relations with others.
  • Time management: It can be a bit difficult to manage and allocate time effectively.

Opportunities

  • School and personal projects and assignments to practice problem solving and work in teams.
  • Internship opportunities at software companies in the Netherlands.
  • Growing demand in IT specialists in software development AI and cybersecurity.

Threats

  • Transportation, which can be delayed or cancelled.
  • A part-time job that takes a lot of time and energy
  • Emergence of some of serious personal problems that can influence me mentally or financially.

Goal 1 (Strength)

By the end of the semester, I want to learn more about software development, the fields of data science and cybersecurity, to better understand these fields and discover which of them interests me more. Measurable: I study and conduct independent research at least 5 hours per week and discuss related topics with others.


Goal 2 (Weakness)

By the end of the semester, I want to improve my time-management skills. Measurable: I will try to set priorities effectively for myself and manage my time based on those priorities.
