Directions

Honestly, I’m still considering whether I’d like to work in a more technically oriented direction or in a people- and organization-oriented one. However, at this point, software development and cybersecurity appeal to me more. I like creating new things and making people’s lives easier. At the same time, I prioritize creating a safe networking environment.

Future me

I will have found a job at a cloud hosting company. Security is essential to me. It is important to be able to control the things you create as well as protect them. I will have learned back-end programming languages such as PHP and Python. I will also have improved my knowledge of front-end technologies, server management, databases and data structures, and cybersecurity.
