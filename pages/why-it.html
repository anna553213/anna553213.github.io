Why did i choose IT?

First time I’ve got an excess to computer in the age of about 8. At that time the only thing that interested me was obviously as of the mediocre kid - to play games and creating funny things in Photoshop or Paint. Later, becoming older I started questioning how does computers and all the application that we use on the daily basis works like. Honestly, it even started to scare me a bit. Because the realization that the thing that has such enormous influence on my life is technically unknown to me. As a result, I started to learn more about the usage of modern technology, AI and different programming tools. Which brought me to this studying program.


During my study I want to become well rounded IT professional that can create programming products of high quality such as application and websites. To do so, I’m going to learn different programming languages such as Java Script and C++ for instance and improve my HTML and CSS skills which will help me with creating sites. And of course, a lot of practice, as I believe is the most important part of the whole studying process. That is the reason I chose this University as well.


Besides, I have more abstract and personal reasons that motivates me. I would like to develop some security systems that would help with defending my country on different frontlines. But it’s more like a dream for now than a concrete goal.
