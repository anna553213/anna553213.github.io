Why did i choose IT?

The first time I got access to a computer was when I was about eight years old. At that time, the only things that interested me, as you might expect from an average kid, were playing games and creating funny things in Photoshop or Paint. Later, as I grew older, I started wondering how computers and the applications we use on a daily basis actually worked. Honestly, this even started to scare me a little because I realized that something with such an enormous influence on my life was still largely unknown to me. As a result, I started learning more about modern technology, AI, and different programming tools, which eventually brought me to this study program.


During my studies, I want to become a well-rounded IT professional who can develop high-quality software products, such as applications and websites. To achieve this, I plan to learn different programming languages, such as JavaScript, C++ and others, and improve my HTML and CSS skills, which will help me create websites. Of course, I also intend to get plenty of practice, as I believe it is the most important part of the entire learning process. That is the reason why I chose this university.


Besides these professional goals, I also have more personal reasons that motivate me. I would like to develop security systems that could help defend my country on different fronts such as informational, for instance. However, for now, this is more of a dream than a concrete goal.
